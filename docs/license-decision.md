# License Decision

Zentra is released under GPL-3.0.

Why:

- Strong copyleft makes privacy regressions visible in distributed forks.
- A permissive license would allow closed-source telemetry forks.
- The project wants outside contributions without a heavy CLA workflow.

Contributor workflow:

- Contributions are accepted with DCO sign-off.
- Contributors retain copyright to their work.
- Contributors grant ModAstera the right to redistribute contributions under additional terms where necessary for platform distribution, including the App Store.

What this does not mean:

- It does not guarantee App Store distribution today.
- It does not grant proprietary relicensing rights to third parties.
- It does not weaken the GPL-3.0 terms for the public source repository.
